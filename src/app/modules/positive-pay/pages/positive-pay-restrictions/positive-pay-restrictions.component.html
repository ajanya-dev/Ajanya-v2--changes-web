Positive Pay 

Positive Pay is a service that helps prevent fraud by notifying your bank about the checks or ACH payments you issue. When someone attempts to cash a check or withdraw money from your account, the bank compares it with the information you've provided. If the details don't match, the bank won't process the payment, keeping your money safe from fraud. To start using Positive Pay, simply ask your bank to set it up. Once it's active, our system works automatically with your bank. There may be a small fee, but it helps protect your transactions.

How Positive Pay Works
  • Click the 'Proceed to Setup' button to access the Positive Pay dashboard.
  • Select the bank account and set up Positive Pay
  • Once configured, the file will be sent to bank every day, ensuring only the checks you write get cashed
  • You can also download your check records for proof of approved payments
