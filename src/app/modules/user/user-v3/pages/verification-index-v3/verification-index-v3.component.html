In the US, various laws and regulations govern the data security and privacy of users, including the CCPA, HIPAA, GLBA, COPPA, and FCRA.

The US government also has agencies such as the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) that provide guidelines and support to businesses for ensuring data security.

Laws for Data Privacy and Protection in USA

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - provides California residents with the right to know what personal information is being collected about them, the right to request deletion of their information, and right to opt-out of the sale of their personal information

The Gramm-Leach-Billey Act (GLBA) - requires financial institutions to protect the privacy of customers' personal information

The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) - regulates the collection, dissemination, and use of consumer credit information.
