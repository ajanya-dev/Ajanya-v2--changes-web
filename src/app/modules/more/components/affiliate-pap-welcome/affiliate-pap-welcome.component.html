Frequently asked questions

What is an affiliate program?

An affiliate program is a marketing strategy where business pay affiliates (people who promote their products or services) a commission for each sale made through their unique referral link.

How much can I earn as an affiliate?

A: Earnings for affiliates vary depending on the commission rate, the number of sales generated, and the price of the product or service being promoted. Some affiliates earn a few dollars per sale, while others earn thousands of dollars per sale, and still, others earn thousands of dollars per month.
