How to verify VOIP Mobile Number?

This article answers “How to verify VOIP Mobile Number?” for the current version of OnlineCheckWriter. If your screen looks different you may be on the Classic layout; the steps are the same, only the menu names differ.

Quick answer

Open General from the left menu, choose the action you need from the toolbar, and confirm. Changes take effect immediately and show up in the activity log.

Steps

  1. Open General

    Sign in and choose General in the left menu. Use the search box at the top if you cannot see it.

  2. Choose the action

    Pick the action from the toolbar or the row menu. Required fields are marked; the form explains any limits that apply to your account.

  3. Review and confirm

    Check the summary and press Confirm. You can track the result from the activity log on the same page.

Good to know

Limits, fees and processing times depend on your plan and verification status. The Pricing topic has the current numbers.
